Data Retention and Deletion Policy

Effective November 26, 2026 · Liam Hosfeld

How long Lima keeps what, where, and how to delete it.

Download PDF

Lima's data lives only on your Mac and stays until you delete it. We hold no copy of your data on any server.

DataWhereKeptHow it's deleted
Notes, chats, workspaces, Companion memory, personal detailsEncrypted store on your MacUntil you delete themDelete them in Lima; trashed notes stay restorable until you empty them from the trash
Recorded activity (for reports and Companion memory)Encrypted store on your Mac90 daysRemoved automatically; private windows are never recorded
Previous app version (for rollback)Your Mac, in Lima's update folderUntil the next updateReplaced at the next update
Logs (runtime.log, update.log)~/Library/Logs/LimaUntil you delete themDelete the files
Data held by your AI provider, search engine, Google or websitesTheir systemsUnder their own policiesThrough each provider

Removing everything

Delete Lima.app, ~/Library/Application Support/Lima, ~/Library/Application Support/Lima Browser and ~/Library/Logs/Lima, then remove Lima's item from your Keychain. Lima doesn't hold a copy anywhere else.

Legal holds

If the law requires us to keep any record longer, we keep only what's required, for as long as it's required.
