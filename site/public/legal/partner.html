A template for future partners; Lima has none today. Bracketed terms are filled in for each partner.

This Agreement is between Liam Hosfeld ("Lima") and [Partner legal name] ("Partner"), effective [date].

1. Scope

Partner will [describe the search arrangement, integration, placement or referral program] (the "Program").

2. Compensation

Lima earns [rate or fee] on [qualifying events], paid [monthly] within [30] days of month end, with a statement of qualifying events. Nothing is earned on refunds, fraud or events Partner can't verify.

3. Disclosure to users

Every sponsored placement, paid ranking and commission-earning link, and any paid search default, is disclosed to users. Lima lists active partnerships on its legal page and in its release notes. Partner will not ask Lima to hide or blur these disclosures.

4. Data

Lima collects no user data and shares none with Partner. For a search partner, searches travel directly from the user's browser to Partner under Partner's own privacy policy; Lima adds no identifier beyond any partner code the Program requires. Partner will not try to identify Lima users through the Program.

5. Security

Each party protects any information it receives with reasonable safeguards and reports a security incident affecting the Program within 72 hours.

6. Brand use

Each party may use the other's name and logo only as approved in writing for the Program.

7. Compliance

Each party complies with applicable law, including consumer protection, advertising disclosure (such as the FTC Endorsement Guides) and privacy laws.

8. Term and termination

[12] months, renewing unless either party gives [30] days' notice. Either party may end it immediately for a material breach not fixed within [15] days of notice.

9. Liability

Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages. Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the prior [12] months, except for breaches of sections 4 and 5.

10. General

Governed by the laws of the State of Georgia, United States. This is the entire agreement; changes must be in writing and signed by both parties.

Signed: Liam Hosfeld, for Lima · [Name, title, date], for [Partner legal name]
