Acceptable Use Policy

Effective November 26, 2026 · Liam Hosfeld

What Lima, and the agents you run in it, may not be used for.

Download PDF

Use Lima, and the agents you run in it, only for lawful purposes. You may not use Lima, directly or through an agent, to:

  1. Break the law, including fraud, theft, money laundering, sanctions violations, or illegal gambling where you live.
  2. Gain unauthorized access to accounts, systems or data, defeat authentication or CAPTCHAs, or test security without the owner's permission (permitted research follows the Vulnerability Disclosure Policy).
  3. Run denial-of-service attacks, mass scraping that breaches a site's terms, spam, or bulk account creation.
  4. Create or spread malware, phishing pages or credential-harvesting flows.
  5. Harass, stalk, threaten or dox anyone, or collect personal data about people without a lawful basis.
  6. Impersonate a person or organization, or deceive people into thinking they're dealing with a human when an agent is acting.
  7. Create or distribute child sexual abuse material, or any content that exploits minors.
  8. Infringe intellectual property, including distributing modified builds under the Lima name or icon.
  9. Breach your AI provider's usage policies or the terms of the services agents use for you.
  10. Interfere with Lima's update or signing services, or tamper with another person's installation.

Enforcement

Lima runs on your Mac, so we can't and don't monitor how it's used. We may refuse support and cooperate with lawful requests from authorities. Report abuse to lima.issues@gmail.com.
