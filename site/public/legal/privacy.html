Liam Hosfeld publishes Lima. We never see your notes, chats, files or browsing: Lima keeps them on your Mac and has no accounts. We collect no usage statistics, analytics or telemetry.

What stays on your Mac

Notes, chats, workspace history, recorded activity, Companion memory and the personal details you enter are kept in an encrypted store whose key is in your macOS Keychain. There is no cloud copy. Lima keeps a week of daily backups of that store on your Mac, still encrypted, and an export you make goes only where you save it. Private windows are never recorded or shown to agents.

Problem reports stay on your Mac too. When you create one in Settings, Lima writes it to a file (recent logs with email addresses, tokens and your home folder removed) and opens an email draft; nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Shared note links carry the note itself after the "#" of a lima.liamhosfeld.com/n link. Browsers don't send that part to our server, so nothing is uploaded; anyone with the link can read the note.

What we never do

We don't sell or share personal data, show ads, build profiles of individuals, or use your content to train models.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off Companion memory, search suggestions, location and each integration in Settings, and delete everything Lima keeps by deleting its data on your Mac. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data or to object to processing. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is usually nothing on our side to act on; contact lima.issues@gmail.com with any request.

Children

Lima isn't directed to children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and UK.

Security

Local data is encrypted at rest, and updates are signature-checked before they install. Report security issues under the Vulnerability Disclosure Policy.

Changes

We announce material changes in the release notes and on this website before they take effect.
