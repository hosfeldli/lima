Lima lets AI agents work in your browser tabs and on your Mac, and you stay in charge of anything consequential.

How agents run

Agents are the Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode command-line tools you install and sign in to. They run on your Mac under your provider account and plan. Lima gives them tools to open and read tabs, write and organize notes, and work in their workspace folder.

What always needs your approval

Lima shows an approval card, and waits, before an agent or an outside AI tool:

  • spends money or acts on an account (purchases, bets, transfers, orders, sending messages, publishing, deleting an account, changing a password);
  • submits forms, enters passwords or payment details, uploads or downloads files, or visits pages outside an approved plan;
  • reads your chats, takes screenshots, opens apps, uses your location, exports keys or changes settings;
  • creates scheduled tasks, or does anything else Lima's policy flags.

Agents may ask at the start of a task for the approvals they expect to need, and those last up to 4 hours for that task. Payments and other irreversible steps still ask at the moment they happen. A chat message saying "approved" never counts as approval.

Agents may not

be used to get around a site's security, rate limits or terms, impersonate people, or act outside the workspace and plan you approved.

Your responsibilities

  • Review what an agent proposes before you approve it. Approval is your decision, and its result is your responsibility.
  • Check AI output before you rely on it. AI can make mistakes, and Lima shows that reminder under every chat.
  • Follow your AI provider's usage policies and the terms of the sites agents use for you.
  • Don't give agents tasks the Acceptable Use Policy prohibits.
  • Keep secrets out of prompts unless the task needs them.

Untrusted content

Web pages and files can contain instructions written to manipulate AI. Lima treats page content as data, but no defense is perfect. Approval cards are your safeguard, so read them.

Automation limits

Agents work in a workspace folder of your choosing and can run commands there. Lima doesn't sandbox commands beyond your Mac's own protections, so run agents only in folders and on tasks you're comfortable with.

Reporting misuse

Report agent behavior that bypassed an approval to lima.issues@gmail.com.
