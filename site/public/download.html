Installing

  1. Open Lima.dmg from your Downloads and drag Lima to Applications.
  2. Open Lima from Applications. It opens like any Mac app; there's no account to create.
  3. Follow the short walkthrough: bring over your browser data, connect an AI if you want one, and try a first task. You can skip any step.

For AI features

Lima uses Claude Code, Codex or OpenCode with your own plan. The walkthrough can install one and open its sign-in, so you don't need the terminal. Without one, Lima is a full browser with notes and search.

Updates

Lima updates itself. Each update is signed and checked before it installs, and a What's New page shows what changed.

Lima is built on Zen and Firefox (MPL-2.0). Published by Liam Hosfeld.
