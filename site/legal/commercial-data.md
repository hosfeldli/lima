---
title: Commercial Data Policy
summary: Lima collects no data to sell; how its search default works and how any partnership would be disclosed.
---
Lima doesn't collect, sell, license or share user data. It has no analytics or telemetry, so there is nothing to commercialize. Lima's commercial model is its preferred search engine.

## The preferred search engine
Google is Lima's default search engine for new installs. You can choose another engine in Settings → Search at any time, and Lima never changes your choice afterwards. Searches go directly from your browser to the engine you use, under that engine's privacy policy; Lima doesn't see or keep them.

If Lima receives payments from a search provider, that arrangement will be disclosed on this page before it starts, naming the provider. No such arrangement exists today.

## What is never commercialized

| Data | Policy |
| --- | --- |
| Browser history | Never collected or commercialized |
| AI conversations | Never collected or commercialized |
| Local source code and files | Never collected or commercialized |
| Screenshots and documents | Never collected or commercialized |
| Activity and individual profiles | Never collected or commercialized |
| Feature use and product analytics | Not collected |

## Partnerships
Sponsored placements, commissions and paid partnerships, including any search partnership, are disclosed where they appear and listed on this page and in the release notes when they start. No partner receives user data.

## Changes
Changes to this policy are announced 30 days ahead.
